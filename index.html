Frequently asked questions

Before you open a ticket.

Practical answers about appeal videos, channel purchases, Gmail delivery and support.

What exactly does Premonly do?
We provide custom reused-content appeal video production, help source YPP-approved channels and offer aged Gmail accounts when available. We handle inquiries through Discord tickets.
Are channels guaranteed to be safe?
No channel or future monetization outcome can be guaranteed. Ask for current channel information, review the relevant terms and perform your own due diligence before any transaction.
Do you guarantee monetization or appeal approval?
No. Every appeal is reviewed by YouTube. We prepare a video and authentic evidence around your channel; a positive decision cannot be promised.
How do buying and selling work?
Start in Discord, describe your requirements, request details about an available opportunity and discuss the payment, ownership and transfer steps before proceeding.
What do you need for an appeal video?
Typically the channel context, the notice from YouTube, examples of your work and genuine editing or production evidence. We'll explain the specific requirements after reviewing the case.
Do you use a generic appeal template?
No. We prepare the script around the channel's real content and editing process. Evidence must reflect the creator's actual work.
What if my reused-content appeal is rejected?
You can discuss what happened and whether your materials or approach need improvement. Any further appeal or application depends on YouTube's rules and eligibility requirements.
How long does delivery take?
Timelines vary with the specific channel, transfer method, verification and both parties' availability. Confirm a realistic timeframe before committing.
Is there support after a deal?
You can ask about post-transaction guidance before purchasing. The scope and duration of any support should be explicitly confirmed for your deal.
Can I sell my own monetized channel?
You can contact Premonly to discuss the channel and potential buyers. Any transfer should follow the relevant platform requirements and the terms agreed by both parties.
Do you sell aged Gmail accounts?
Yes, subject to current stock. Ask us to confirm the account details, delivery process, any platform restrictions and the exact replacement policy before purchasing.
How do I check current stock or open a ticket?
Join our Discord using the links on this site and follow the server's ticket instructions. Availability changes, so please confirm it directly.
