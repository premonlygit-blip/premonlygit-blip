06 / Good questions, clear answers

Before you decide.

Answers to the questions creators often ask about channels, appeals and the process.

What exactly does Premonly do?
Premonly helps creators explore monetized YouTube channels, discuss buying or selling arrangements, and get guidance preparing reused-content monetization appeals.
Are channels guaranteed to be safe?
No channel or future monetization outcome can be guaranteed. Ask for current channel information, review the relevant terms and perform your own due diligence before any transaction.
Do you guarantee monetization or appeal approval?
No. Decisions about monetization and appeals are made by YouTube under its policies. Premonly offers preparation and guidance, not a guarantee of a particular result.
How do buying and selling work?
Start in Discord, describe your requirements, request details about an available opportunity and discuss the payment, ownership and transfer steps before proceeding.
What if my reused-content appeal is rejected?
You can discuss what happened and whether your materials or approach need improvement. Any further appeal or application depends on YouTube's rules and eligibility requirements.
How long does delivery take?
Timelines vary with the specific channel, transfer method, verification and both parties' availability. Confirm a realistic timeframe before committing.
Is there support after a deal?
You can ask about post-transaction guidance before purchasing. The scope and duration of any support should be explicitly confirmed for your deal.
Can I sell my own monetized channel?
You can contact Premonly to discuss the channel and potential buyers. Any transfer should follow the relevant platform requirements and the terms agreed by both parties.
